About Our Project

Diet & Nutrition Planner is an AI-powered web application developed to help users maintain a healthy lifestyle through personalized diet planning and image-based food recognition.

The system calculates BMI, provides customized diet recommendations, analyzes nutritional values, and identifies food items using YOLOv8 technology.
